What is SongHub?

SongHub extends uvlhub.io into an extensible Open Science platform for both musical telemetry datasets and UVL feature models integrated with Zenodo and FlamaPy.

Can I contribute?

Sure! Fork our repository on GitHub, build new features or dataset domains, and contribute back.
